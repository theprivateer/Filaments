# Australian store collection audit

Collection: [Bambu Lab AU filament collection](https://au.store.bambulab.com/collections/bambu-lab-3d-printer-filament)

Checked: 2 October 2026

Review status: draft

The live collection contained 52 listing cards across five pages. Forty-two cards linked to distinct filament material pages. The other ten linked to bundles, trial packs, a reusable spool, a backlight board or filament swatches, so they don't create distinct material-and-colour catalogue entries.

Every material page is represented in `au-store-prototype.json`. The snapshot has 43 product records because the combined TPU 85A / TPU 90A page maps to two material variants.
